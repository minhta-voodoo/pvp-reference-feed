How it works

Steam games worth borrowing from for mobile PvP battle games. Notion is the source of truth; this page is a read-only view of it.

What qualifies

Visual & layout
A battlefield or screen layout, camera or art direction that could frame a mobile battle game. E.g. a side-on lane with interactive terrain, a one-screen island, stacked floors, a 3×3 grid, a moving base.
PvP mechanic
A rule, resource or interaction that could anchor a mobile PvP game. E.g. drafting, deploy and lane systems, positioning, counters, tempo and economy, simultaneous turns, chance devices.
Layout + mechanic
Has both: a new layout and a PvP mechanic worth borrowing.
Theme & art
A breakout indie that isn't PvP material itself, but has a setting, premise or art style that could wrap a mobile battle game.
  • Layout and mechanic finds must name something specifically new. Skipped: genre-standard games, pure narrative, atmosphere-only horror, precision real-time input that won't simplify for touch, and sequels that inherit their core unchanged.
  • Theme & art finds need one verified number (≥10K Steam followers, ≥250K units sold, or ≥10K all-time peak players), a breakout in the last ~12 months, and an ad-safe theme.
  • Novelty is judged against the whole library, not against any single game. Numbers that can't be verified are left out.

Where finds come from

  • Steam's most-wishlisted lists for 11 battle-relevant tags: Strategy, Auto Battler, Tower Defense, Roguelike Deckbuilder, RTS, Card Battler, Turn-Based Tactics, Base Building, Party Game, Action Roguelike, Board Game.
  • A watchlist of 25 indie publishers (Hooded Horse, Raw Fury, Devolver Digital, Klei, Polden and others): new releases, announcements and demos.
  • Indie-wide Steam lists for Theme & art: trending new releases, top sellers, most wishlisted and upcoming.
  • GameDiscoverCo, SteamDB's upcoming releases, and the monthly Rogueliker and Turn Based Lovers roundups.
  • Each candidate is checked against its Steam data (release, publisher, demo, platforms), follower count and reviews.

Traction

  • Steam follower counts are re-read by the scan: weekly for active titles (unreleased, demo, Early Access, Theme & art, or released in the last ~6 months), about monthly for older ones.
  • Per week is the follower change between the last two readings, scaled to 7 days. A reading more than 20% below the previous one is treated as a bad read and skipped.
  • Hot ≥1,000 new followers a week · Big ≥10K followers · Mid ≥2K · Small below 2K · Unknown not read yet.
